SECTION 17: HOW TO FIND SPECIFIC CHAPTER PDFs
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FOR NCERT (Best deep-link support):
  1. Know the book code (e.g., lehs1 = Class 12 History Part I)
  2. Full book: https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/{book_code}.pdf
  3. Chapter: https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/{book_code}{ch_no}.pdf (ch_no = 01, 02...)

FOR OTHER PORTALS:
  Most portals (UPSC, WBPSC, NPTEL, e-GyanKosh) don't support direct chapter PDF URLs.
  Use this workflow:
  1. Go to portal URL listed above
  2. Navigate to the specific material
  3. Use browser "Save as PDF" or portal's "Download" button
  4. For repeated access, bookmark the specific page

FOR OPENSTAX:
  1. Go to book page
  2. Click "Download a PDF" → Full book PDF
  3. For chapters: Open PDF → Use PDF reader bookmarks/thumbnails
