HOW TO USE THIS DIRECTORY
------------------------
Every examination entry lists:
  [DOC]          Official documentation gateway - notifications, eligibility,
                 exam pattern, syllabus and official candidate roadmaps.
  [PYQ]          Official previous year question papers, sample papers, or keys
                 (direct PDF links provided wherever publicly hosted).
  [DIRECT PDF]   Direct 1-click downloads of full-text textbooks, bare acts,
                 formula handbooks, and official question booklets.
  [CHAPTER PDF]  Direct 1-click downloads for specific chapters, high-yield
                 units, and foundational modules.
  [OER]          Free & open-licensed educational repositories (government,
                 university, or non-profit - zero login, zero paywall).
